Writing a self-review can be a great opportunity to reflect on your accomplishments, set goals for the future, and demonstrate your value to your organization. Here's a general outline to help you get started:

**Introduction**

* Start by introducing yourself and your role within the company.
* Mention the time period being reviewed (e.g., January 1 - December 31).

**Accomplishments**

* List your key accomplishments and achievements from the past year. Be specific and provide examples.
* Quantify your achievements wherever possible (e.g., "increased sales by 25%," "reduced project timelines by 30%").
* Emphasize how your accomplishments have positively impacted the company, such as:
	+ Improving processes or efficiency
	+ Enhancing customer satisfaction
	+ Contributing to revenue growth
	+ Developing new skills or expertise

**Challenges and Areas for Improvement**

* Discuss any challenges you faced during the year and how you overcame them.
* Identify areas where you'd like to improve or develop new skills.
* Be honest and self-reflective, but also focus on what you've learned and how you plan to apply those lessons in the future.

**Goals and Objectives**

* Outline your goals and objectives for the upcoming year.
* Make sure these goals are specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART).
* Align your goals with the company's overall objectives and priorities.

**Professional Development**

* Describe any training, courses, or conferences you've attended during the year.
* Mention any new skills or certifications you've acquired.
* Discuss how you plan to continue developing your skills and expertise in the future.

**Conclusion**

* Summarize your key accomplishments and takeaways from the past year.
* Reiterate your commitment to the company and your role.
* End with a positive note, expressing your enthusiasm for the upcoming year and your confidence in your ability to make significant contributions.
